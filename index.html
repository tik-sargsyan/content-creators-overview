05 · Get access & connect

From zero to your first render, in about 5 minutes

Get the repo, hand it to Claude, connect Picsart. The creator-setup skill walks you through all of it, just say "set me up".

1. Get the repo with your token

Tik gives you a GitLab access token (glpat-...). In Claude Code, paste it and say "clone the content-creators repo with this token", Claude clones it for you. Keep the token private.

2. Hand it to Claude

Install Claude Code, open the cloned folder, say run creator setup. Claude reads the kit and does the rest.

3. Connect both plugins

Flow: /plugin install picsart-flow@picsart-flow. Presets: the Picsart GenAI plugin. Both use one Picsart sign-in, no keys.

4. Sign in with Picsart

On first use you sign in with your own account. That is your credits, and every render asks first.

Then just ask, for example: "Build me a product launch ad from this bottle photo", or "run the Selfie to Pro Photoshoot preset." Stuck? Say "verify my setup".
