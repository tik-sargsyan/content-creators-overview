01 · The goal

Zero to finished content, guided all the way

Creators ask in plain words and get real results: films, ads, cartoons, product shots, avatars. The kit brings the recipes and the safety rails; the creator brings the idea.

In one line

Great output, no engineering

Everyone clones the same kit and works on their own account. Only the owner edits it, so the whole team stays in sync.

The one rule

  • Runs on your Picsart sign-in and your credits.
  • Every render asks you first. Nothing spends without your OK.
  • No API keys, ever. If a step asks for one, something's wrong.
  • The private internal system stays separate and hidden.

03 · The two tracks

Two ways to create, matched to the job

Flow for bigger, multi-step projects. Presets for a quick one-step result. Same sign-in, same kit.

Track A · Flow

Bigger, multi-step projects

Several steps combine into one finished piece, built from ready blueprints.

  • Children's Content & Cartoons · kids cartoons to publish
  • Filmmaking & Storytelling · short films and short-form
  • Marketing & Content · ads, UGC, launches
  • E-commerce & Brand Growth · product visuals and catalogs
  • Design & Creative Production · editing and brand assets

Ready blueprints: Short Film, Product Ad, Kids Cartoon, Brand-in-a-Box, Creator UGC Kit.

Track B · Presets

Quick, one-step results

One input, one preset, one result. Start from a recipe, swap in your subject.

  • Portraits & Avatars · avatars, headshots
  • Photo Editing · fix and elevate a photo
  • Products & E-commerce · clean product shots
  • Stickers & Illustrations · sticker sheets, icons
  • Fashion · lookbook shots
  • Branding & Logos · a quick logo

Six simple lanes, each with recipes to copy.

Which one? One thing in one step goes to Presets. A story or campaign with several pieces goes to Flow.
