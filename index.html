01 · The idea

One kit. Two ways to work.

A creator asks in plain words and gets a real result: an ad, a cartoon, a product shot, an avatar. The kit brings the recipes and the safety rails; the creator brings the idea. Everything runs on the creator's own Picsart sign-in and credits, and every render asks before it spends.

What you run

The creator kit (on demand)

You clone the repo and chat with Claude. It uses the Flow plugin and ready blueprints to build for you. No automation, no dashboards, just you and Claude on your account.

  • Flow track for multi-step projects (films, ads, cartoons).
  • Presets track for quick one-shot results (avatars, product shots).
  • Runs only when you ask. Nothing scheduled, nothing shared.
What runs on the owner's machine

The automation (owner only)

A private system on the owner's computer. It creates and measures templates for the whole program. Creators do not run or need this.

  • Daily agent: builds and renders one new template a day, drops the review link in Slack for approval.
  • Weekly analyzer: pulls live performance from the data warehouse and posts a scorecard.
  • Needs the owner's Flow, warehouse, and Slack access. Kept separate.

04 · Get access & connect

From zero to your first render, in about 5 minutes

Get the repo, hand it to your Claude, connect Picsart. The creator-setup skill walks you through all of it, just say "set me up".

1. Get the repo

Request Reporter access to the GitLab repo, then Code → Download the zip (or clone it). No Git knowledge needed to download.

2. Hand it to Claude

Install Claude Code (desktop, VS Code, or terminal), open the downloaded folder, and say run creator setup. Claude reads the kit and does the rest.

3. Connect Flow

/plugin marketplace add PicsArt/flow-claude-plugin then /plugin install picsart-flow@picsart-flow. This adds the Flow tools and build skills.

4. Sign in with Picsart

On first use, Flow asks you to sign in with your own Picsart account. That is your credits. Presets and GenAI use the same sign-in, no keys anywhere.

Then just ask, for example: "Build me a product launch ad from this bottle photo." Stuck later? Say "verify my setup" and it re-checks everything.
